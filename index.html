CASINO VIP SALON RULES

International Casino Standards

🃏 Baccarat (Punto Banco)

• Card Values: Aces = 1, 2–9 = Face value, 10/J/Q/K = 0. Rightmost digit is the total.

• Naturals: 8 or 9 on initial 2 cards ends round immediately.

• Payouts: Player 1:1, Banker 0.95:1 (5% commission), Tie 8:1, Pairs 11:1.

♠️ Blackjack (Vegas Strip Rules)

• Goal: Beat Dealer's hand without exceeding 21. Aces count as 1 or 11.

• Blackjack (Ace + 10-val): Pays 3:2 immediately unless Dealer also has Blackjack.

• Dealer Rule: Dealer must draw to 16 and stands on all 17s.

• Double Down: Double your wager on initial two cards and receive exactly 1 card.

• Split: When dealt two cards of identical rank, split into two separate hands.

• Insurance: Pays 2:1 if Dealer shows an Ace and has Blackjack.
